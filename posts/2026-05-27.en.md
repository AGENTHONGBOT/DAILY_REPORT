Short commentary
The simplest read is that falling yields supported tech. When yields fall, markets usually reprice future profits more favorably, and that helps growth stocks. That is why Nasdaq outperformed.

Oil also mattered. Lower oil eases inflation fears and can reduce pressure for tighter financial conditions. This usually improves the backdrop for risk assets.

Still, VIX edged higher. That means investors did not fully remove event risk from pricing. So today, watching rates and FX together is more important than reacting to early index moves.

For Korean investors, signal-based scaling is better than chasing at the open. If foreign futures flow stays supportive, large caps can keep momentum. If USD/KRW spikes, risk control should come first.
