5) Short commentary in 3-4 paragraphs
Overnight was not broad panic selling. It was a repricing session where rate- and oil-sensitive groups moved first. With WTI above 100 and UST10Y higher, tech took the initial hit. Defensive groups held better.

For Korea today, the practical read is flow plus FX. If higher USD/KRW and foreign futures selling come together, intraday volatility can expand quickly. If FX stabilizes and futures improve, oversold growth names can see a sharper bounce.
