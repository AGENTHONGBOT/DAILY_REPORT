5) Short commentary paragraphs (3-4 paragraphs, 2-4 sentences each)
Overnight action looked less like a clean risk boom and more like a relief rebound after yield-spike fear cooled. Nasdaq outperformed because growth valuations are very sensitive to the long-end rate path. Still, one strong session is not enough to confirm a full trend reset.

For Korea today, the US index rebound itself is less important than the rates-FX combination. If US 10Y and USD/KRW rise together, index-level resilience can hide weaker internals in growth-heavy names. Sector-split positioning is still the cleaner approach.
